How to Protect Your Online Privacy

Learn simple ways to protect your personal information and stay safer while using the internet.

Use Strong Passwords

Create strong and unique passwords for your online accounts.

Avoid Suspicious Links

Do not click suspicious links or download files from unknown sources.

Protect Personal Information

Avoid sharing sensitive personal information with unknown people or websites.

Use Secure Websites

Look for HTTPS when visiting websites and avoid entering personal information on unsafe pages.

Keep Your Accounts Safe

Use two-factor authentication when it is available and keep your account information secure.

Conclusion

Simple online safety habits can help protect your privacy and personal information.
